**Samet Funds** are owner-operated, single-asset lending pools: the owner funds a pool and sets a fee; borrowers draw from it and repay with that fee attached. A companion user flow lets borrowers take the loan, swap it into the asset they actually want on the DEX, and repay — atomically, in one transaction.

## Two sides of one pool

- **Owner** — funds the pool with a backing asset, sets the fee rate, tops up or changes the fee later, and can delete the fund. Earnings are the accumulated fees; the dashboard shows available balance net of unpaid amounts and warns on unusually high fees.
- **Borrower** — draws a reference asset, immediately swaps it into the target asset via a limit order, and repays the fund. All three legs (`samet_fund_borrow` + `limit_order_create` + `samet_fund_repay`) bundle atomically: everything succeeds or nothing does.

## Samet Funds vs credit offers

Both are peer-to-peer lending, but shaped differently: a Samet Fund is one standing pool with one fee that anyone may draw from, while [credit offers](/docs/learn/credit.html) are discrete proposals with per-offer collateral terms, durations, and borrower whitelists. Use a fund for simple always-on liquidity; use credit offers for negotiated, collateralized loans.
