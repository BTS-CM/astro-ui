Voting weight comes from stake — and **tickets** let you lock BTS to multiply yours. The longer the lock, the bigger the boost, up to 8×. Locked tickets also underpin worker-proposal campaigning: backers lock to amplify the votes that fund the work.

## Lock types and boosts

| Lock | Boost |
| ---- | ----- |
| Liquid | 1× |
| 180 days | 2× |
| 360 days | 4× |
| 720 days / forever | 8× |

Create offers the three fixed locks; updating additionally allows moving to liquid. Forever tickets are frozen — they cannot be changed afterwards. Effective power (locked × boost) is what the [leaderboard](/ticket_leaderboard.html) ranks.

## THUMBSUP.1

On mainnet, every locked BTS must be matched by a THUMBSUP.1 balance — a token issued by the `abit` account that tracks ticket coverage. Short of it? The ticket screen builds a proposal asking `abit` to transfer you the deficit (capped by your BTS balance), so coverage and locking stay in step.

## Using tickets politically

- **Amplify yourself** before publishing or backing a [worker proposal](/docs/learn/workers.html) — boosted votes rank it above the refund blockers.
- **Read the room** on the [leaderboard](/docs/governance/ticket_leaderboard.html): it names exactly whose approval decides outcomes, so appeals (and competing locks) can be targeted.
