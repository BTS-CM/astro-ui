A **Hashed Timelock Contract (HTLC)** locks funds behind two simultaneous conditions: knowing a secret, and acting before a deadline. It is the primitive behind cross-chain atomic swaps and trustless conditional escrow — no arbitrator required.

## The two locks

- **Hash lock** — the sender commits to `hash(hash(secret))` on-chain (double SHA-256 or RIPEMD160). Only someone who learns the original secret can claim the funds, by revealing it in a `htlc_redeem`.
- **Time lock** — a `claim_period_seconds` window (minimum 60 seconds, with 1-hour to 30-day presets). If nobody redeems in time, the sender reclaims via refund — funds are never stuck.

## The atomic-swap pattern

Alice and Bob want to trade across chains without trusting each other (or an exchange):

1. Alice locks her coins in an HTLC keyed to `hash(hash(S))`, where only she knows `S`, with a long deadline.
2. Bob verifies Alice's lock, then locks his coins in a matching HTLC for the same hash with a *shorter* deadline.
3. Alice claims Bob's coins by revealing `S` — which publishes `S` on-chain.
4. Bob reads `S` from Alice's claim and uses it to claim Alice's coins before his deadline.

Either both legs complete or both refund. No step lets one side steal: claiming *is* revealing, and revealing enables the other claim.

## Lifecycle in this app

Create (`htlc_create`) → track on the sender/receiver dashboards → recipient redeems (the dialog double-hashes your entered secret and only enables redeem on a hash **Match**) or the sender extends the window (`htlc_extend`, adding seconds). Expired unclaimed contracts refund to the sender.
