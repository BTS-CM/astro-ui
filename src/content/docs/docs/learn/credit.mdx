**Credit** is peer-to-peer lending with on-chain enforcement: lenders publish offers stating asset, fee, acceptable collateral, and terms; borrowers lock collateral and draw funds; the chain tracks the resulting deal through repayment. Unlike margin borrowing (debt minted against SmartCoins), credit moves *existing* assets from lender to borrower.

## Lifecycle: offer → accept → deal → repay

1. **Offer** — a lender publishes asset, balance, fee rate (chain units: `10000` = 1%), max duration, minimum deal size, accepted collateral (assets + prices), an optional borrower whitelist with per-account caps, and an auto-disable time. Offers can be edited (`delta_amount` is relative) or deleted outright.
2. **Accept** — a borrower picks an offer, sets amount and collateral (auto-computed from the offer price, blocked when uncovered), chooses an auto-repay mode (none / full-only / allow-partial), and accepts.
3. **Deal** — the live loan. The borrower repays (`credit_deal_repay`); narrow deal terms like auto-repay can be updated by the borrower.
4. **End** — repaid in full, or the offer disabled/deleted by the lender when no longer wanted.

## Credit vs margin borrowing

| | Credit (this page) | Margin ([Borrow](/docs/debt/borrow.html)) |
|---|---|---|
| Source of funds | A specific lender's balance | Newly minted SmartCoins |
| Collateral | Terms per offer, priced by lender | Protocol MCR/SQPR rules |
| Interest | Lender-set fee rate | No interest — but margin-call risk |
| Counterparty | Named lender | The network |
