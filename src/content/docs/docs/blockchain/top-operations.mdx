**Top Operations** ranks on-chain operation types by frequency and shows each type's share of chain activity — rare types (under 1%) are grouped as Other. It's a read-only window into what the chain actually does all day.

**Open in the app:** [Top Operations](/top-operations.html).

## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Ranking" icon="list">
    Operation types ordered by usage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Breakdown" icon="chart">
    Share of each type, with sub-1% grouped as Other.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read-only" icon="eye">
    Nothing here broadcasts.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How to use

<Steps>

1. Open **Top Operations** from the homepage under *Blockchain Overviews*.

2. Browse the ranked list and the share chart.

3. Use it to sanity-check what activity dominates the chain before designing around an operation.

</Steps>
