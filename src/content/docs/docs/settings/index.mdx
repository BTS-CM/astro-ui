## Operations broadcast by this section

- `account_upgrade` — upgrade to Lifetime Member.
- `account_create` — register a new account (LTM method).
- `account_update` — rotate account keys (Change Password).

`Blocked Users`, `Nodes`, `Configure Visuals`, `Theme Customizer` and `Per-Page Themes` are local-only and never broadcast. On-chain whitelist/blacklist management lives under [Account Overviews](/docs/account/account_lists.html).
